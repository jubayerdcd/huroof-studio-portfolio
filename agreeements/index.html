Designer's copyright notice: The designer retains the original copyright of the creative work under applicable law. This means if any other person or designer copies, claims, or reproduces this logo as their own work, the designer holds legal proof of authorship and can take action. The License Certificate issued to the client serves as mutual proof of this specific original creation.

The designer may display the final logo in their portfolio and social media as a work sample, unless the client requests confidentiality in writing.

⚑ Strongly recommended

A design license alone does not protect your logo against others using a similar mark. The designer strongly encourages the client to register the logo as a Trademark as soon as possible through their country's trademark office (e.g. BIPO in Bangladesh, USPTO in the USA). Trademark registration gives you the legal right to stop others from using a similar name or logo in your industry — something a design license alone cannot do.
