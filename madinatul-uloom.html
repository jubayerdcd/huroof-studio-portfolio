Concept:

The dome shape in the logo represents the Holy Mosque in Madinah, which is considered one of the holiest sites in Islam. The shape of the rehal is also reminiscent of a book, representing the importance of learning and knowledge in Islam.

Inside the dome, you will see Arabic calligraphy, which adds a traditional and cultural element to the design. The calligraphy represents the teachings and values that the madrasah is committed to imparting to its students.

About:

Madinatul Uloom is a religious school in New York City that offers Islamic education to students, and it is dedicated to providing a comprehensive Islamic education to its students.

The curriculum of the Islamic Madrasah is designed to impart knowledge of the Quran, Hadith, Islamic history, Arabic language, and Islamic law (Fiqh). It offers three programs: Hifz/Nadhira Course for Quran memorization, Alim Course for Islamic sciences, and After School Maktab for Kids program for comprehensive Islamic education. With highly qualified faculty, a comprehensive curriculum, and a supportive community.
