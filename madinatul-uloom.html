Madinatul Uloom Brand Identity

Spreading Islamic knowledge, faith, and values in the Western world

The Mission

Madinatul Uloom began with a clear mission:

  • Guide youth with knowledge, faith, and values.
  • An Islamic school and center for community.
  • Built to serve Muslims in USA.

They needed a brand identity that went beyond a simple logo. They needed a true face for their mission—an identity that reflects Islam, clarity, and purpose.

Design Direction

The core of my design strategy was combining Islamic roots with modern clarity. The goal was to stay away from heavy traditional patterns or complex shapes. Instead, the design is simple, memorable, and deeply purposeful.

  • No heavy patterns
  • No complex shapes
  • Rooted in Islamic heritage with a modern twist

Crafting the Identity

Symbolism

  • The dome shape and the book symbol represents a roof of knowledge and faith.
  • The calligraphy in the dome shape helps to connect with Muslim audience.

Color Palette

Deep Green represents growth, peace, and rich Islamic tradition.
Gold signifies excellence, trust, and the dignity of the institution.

Typography & Layout

The chosen fonts and layout are intentionally clean and highly legible, ensuring they remain respectful and supportive of the overarching message.

Final Outcome & Impact

  • Strong First Impression: Instantly conveys an authentic Islamic vibe.
  • Community Connection: Deeply resonates with the Muslim audience in the USA.
  • Aesthetic Appeal: Delivers a modern, clean, and spiritual feel.
  • Meaningful Design: The dome and calligraphy perfectly balance faith and learning.
  • Versatility: Consistent and striking across both print and digital mediums.
